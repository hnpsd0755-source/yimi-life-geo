# Opportunity Scoring

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. Use half-points when needed.

## Dimensions

- Problem intensity: How painful, frequent, and urgent is the user problem?
- Market signal: Are there visible demand signals such as search demand, sales rank, reviews, social discussion, or category growth?
- Differentiation room: Can YIMILIFE offer a clear reason to choose this over alternatives?
- Feasibility: Can the first version be built, sourced, tested, and launched with reasonable complexity?
- Margin potential: Is there room for healthy gross margin after BOM, packaging, logistics, duties, marketing, and returns?
- Channel fit: Does the product fit YIMILIFE's likely sales channels and content/SEO strategy?
- Brand fit: Does the product strengthen YIMILIFE's long-term positioning rather than dilute it?
- Risk level: Regulatory, safety, quality, IP, return-rate, or support risk. Reverse-score this: 5 means low risk, 1 means high risk.

## Recommendation Bands

- 32-40: Strong Go. Move into validation and early product definition.
- 24-31: Conditional Go. Identify the weakest 2 dimensions and validate before committing major resources.
- 16-23: Hold. Research further or narrow the segment.
- 8-15: No-Go unless there is strategic value not captured by the score.

## Output Format

When scoring, include:

- Score table
- Weighted interpretation if some dimensions matter more for this product
- Top 3 reasons to proceed
- Top 3 reasons to stop or delay
- Fastest validation test
- Decision needed from the user
