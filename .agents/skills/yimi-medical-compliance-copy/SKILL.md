# YimiLife Medical Compliance Copy

If this skill conflicts with repository AGENTS.md, follow AGENTS.md first.

Use this skill for YimiLife medical device website copy, product copy, SEO copy, manuals, metadata, schema text, and conversion sections.

## Core Principle

Use professional, credible, restrained B2B medical device manufacturer language. Do not make unsupported clinical, regulatory, diagnostic, preventive, treatment, or guaranteed-performance claims.

## High-Risk Terms To Avoid Unless Explicitly Confirmed

- FDA approved.
- Clinically proven.
- Guaranteed accuracy.
- Diagnose disease.
- Prevent SIDS.
- 100% reliable.
- Zero bias.
- Hospital-grade diagnosis.
- Cure, treat, or prevent disease.

See `references/medical-copy-risk-terms.md` for review guidance.

## Safer Copy Direction

Prefer language about:

- Manufacturer capability.
- Quality system discipline.
- Certification experience and support.
- OEM/ODM customization.
- Delivery stability.
- Calibration workflow.
- Risk reduction for regulatory, quality, and supply-chain evaluation.

## Required Checks

1. Identify claims related to certification, clinical validation, diagnosis, treatment, prevention, infant monitoring, AI advice, algorithm accuracy, skin tone fairness, continuous blood pressure, or physiological indicators.
2. Mark unsupported or ambiguous claims.
3. Replace risky wording with restrained alternatives.
4. Ask the user before asserting certification status, approval status, clinical validation, launch status, mass production status, or medical outcome claims.

## Output Format

Provide:

- Risky phrase.
- Why it is risky.
- Safer replacement.
- Whether user confirmation is required.
