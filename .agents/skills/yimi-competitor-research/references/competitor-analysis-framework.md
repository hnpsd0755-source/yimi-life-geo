# Competitor Analysis Framework

## 1. Hero Positioning

- What buyer segment is addressed?
- Is the company positioned as manufacturer, brand, distributor, or technology provider?
- Are factory, certification, OEM/ODM, or delivery claims visible above the fold?

## 2. Product Architecture

- How are categories grouped?
- Are product pages shallow catalog pages or detailed procurement pages?
- Are specs, certifications, downloads, and inquiry paths easy to find?

## 3. OEM/ODM Capability

- Is customization clearly explained?
- Are tooling, packaging, branding, firmware, app, or regulatory support mentioned?
- Is the process concrete or generic?

## 4. Compliance Expression

- What certifications are mentioned?
- Are claims restrained or risky?
- Is evidence shown through certificates, downloads, model lists, or quality-system language?

## 5. CTA and Conversion

- What is the primary CTA?
- Are inquiry paths repeated at key decision points?
- Does the page reduce buyer uncertainty before asking for contact?

## 6. Visual Style

- Does the page feel like a real manufacturer, a consumer brand, or a template?
- Are photos, tables, cards, and icons consistent?
- Does mobile layout preserve buyer scanning efficiency?

## 7. YimiLife Recommendation

For each finding, convert it into:

- A specific YimiLife page or section recommendation.
- A minimal implementation direction.
- Any facts that must be confirmed before publishing.
