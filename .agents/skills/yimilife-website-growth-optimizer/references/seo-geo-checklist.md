# SEO And GEO Checklist

## SEO

- Search intent: informational, commercial, transactional, navigational
- Primary keyword
- Secondary keywords
- Related entities
- Page title under about 60 characters when possible
- Meta description under about 155 characters when possible
- One clear H1
- H2/H3 structure that answers search intent
- Internal links to product, category, support, and content pages
- Descriptive image alt text
- Canonical URL if relevant
- Structured data where relevant
- Fast, mobile-friendly page experience
- Original expertise or proof that competitors do not have

## GEO

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization: making pages easier for AI search engines and answer engines to understand, cite, and summarize accurately.

Include:

- Clear entity names and product category terms
- Concise "what it is" definition
- "Who it is for" and "who it is not for"
- Comparison tables
- FAQ with direct answers
- Specs and measurable claims
- Use-case examples
- Source-worthy explanations
- Consistent terminology across pages
- Author/company credibility signals

Avoid:

- Unsupported superlatives
- Vague claims like "best quality" without proof
- Thin pages with only images and slogans
- Hidden critical specs
- Inconsistent naming

## SEO/GEO Brief Output

- Page goal
- Audience and intent
- Primary keyword
- Secondary keywords
- Entity list
- Suggested URL
- Title tag
- Meta description
- H1
- H2 outline
- FAQ
- Schema recommendations
- Internal links
- GEO summary block
- Content gaps to fill
