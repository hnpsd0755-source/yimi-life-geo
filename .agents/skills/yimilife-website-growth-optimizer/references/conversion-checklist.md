# Conversion Checklist

## Five-Second Test

The first viewport should answer:

- What is this?
- Who is it for?
- Why should I care?
- Why should I trust YIMILIFE?
- What should I click next?

## CTA

- One primary CTA per page section
- CTA label should be specific: "Request Quote", "View Products", "Get Sample", "Compare Models"
- Keep secondary CTA visibly lower priority
- Repeat CTA after proof and FAQ sections

## Trust

Use relevant proof:

- Certifications
- Warranty
- Return policy
- Manufacturing capability
- Testing process
- Customer reviews
- Distributor/partner logos
- Case studies
- Real product photos and videos

## Objection Handling

Address:

- Price
- Quality
- Safety
- Compatibility
- Installation or setup
- Shipping and delivery
- Warranty
- After-sales support
- Minimum order quantity if B2B
- Customization if B2B

## Form And Funnel

- Ask only for fields needed at this stage
- Show what happens after submission
- Make error states clear
- Track submit, click, scroll, and product-detail events
- Use thank-you pages for campaign measurement

## Prioritization

Rank CRO fixes by:

- Expected impact
- Ease of implementation
- Confidence
- Time to learn
