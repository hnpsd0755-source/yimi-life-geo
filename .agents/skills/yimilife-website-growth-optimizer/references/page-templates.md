## Homepage

1. Hero
   - H1
   - Subheadline
   - Primary CTA
   - Secondary CTA
   - Hero visual direction

2. Trust bar
   - Certifications
   - Customer types
   - Review/rating proof
   - Media/partner proof

3. Product/category entry
   - Category cards
   - Use cases
   - Buying paths

4. Differentiation
   - Why YIMILIFE
   - Design, quality, service, supply chain, innovation

5. Featured product or solution
   - Main value proposition
   - Benefits
   - Specs or proof

6. Social proof and objection handling
   - Testimonials
   - FAQ
   - Warranty/support

7. Final CTA
