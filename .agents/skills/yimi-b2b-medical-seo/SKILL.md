# YimiLife B2B Medical SEO

If this skill conflicts with repository AGENTS.md, follow AGENTS.md first.

Use this skill for YimiLife SEO and GEO work on pages, metadata, internal links, headings, content briefs, product pages, OEM/ODM pages, and blog or landing page planning.

## Fixed Business Facts

Use only the fixed facts in `references/yimi-seo-rules.md` unless the user explicitly confirms an update.

## SEO Direction

YimiLife pages should help overseas B2B buyers quickly judge:

- Whether YimiLife is a real factory.
- Whether YimiLife has certification experience.
- Whether YimiLife supports OEM/ODM.
- Whether YimiLife has stable production capacity.
- Whether YimiLife can reduce regulatory, quality, and delivery risks.

## Required Review Areas

1. Search intent and target buyer.
2. H1 and heading hierarchy.
3. Title and meta description.
4. Primary and secondary keywords without stuffing.
5. Internal links to relevant product, OEM/ODM, technology, and contact pages.
6. Conversion path and CTA clarity.
7. Compliance-safe language.
8. GEO readability for AI search summaries.

## Constraints

- Do not invent certification status, clinical validation, launch status, mass production status, or customer names.
- Do not write consumer-advertising copy.
- Keep claims specific, restrained, and supported by page content.
