PROJECT PREPARATION

From requirements to a confirmed scope.

Use these checkpoints to prepare for an OEM or ODM discussion. The model, development work and next steps are reviewed for your project.

01

Define the brief.

Your input: Product or model, target market and customization needs.

Confirm together: An existing OEM model or the scope of an ODM feasibility review.

02

Review the options.

Your input: Branding, artwork, configuration or development requirements.

Confirm together: The options to assess, required inputs and sample or engineering work.

03

Review the sample.

Your input: Feedback on artwork, configuration or the engineering sample.

Confirm together: The agreed reference and any changes needed before the next stage.

04

Plan the next stage.

Your input: Order, packaging, documentation and shipping requirements.

Confirm together: Outstanding project approvals and production or delivery planning conditions.

Before you enquire.

Should I choose OEM or ODM?

Start with OEM for branding and available configuration options on an existing model. Choose an ODM discussion for a new or modified appearance, structure or function. Compare our pulse oximeters and blood pressure monitors to identify a starting point.

What should I prepare for the first discussion?

Share the product or model, intended users, target market and key requirements. An estimated quantity, target schedule and reference artwork help clarify the scope; you can leave optional details open if they are not decided. Use Contact if you are unsure which path fits.

How are MOQ and project timing confirmed?

Minimum order quantities and sample, development or production timing depend on the selected model, configuration and project scope. Share your expected quantity and target schedule so the applicable conditions can be reviewed.

What does the configuration preview confirm?

The example below illustrates colors and logo placement for discussion. It does not confirm model availability, a quotation or production approval. In this review version, logo files, project attachments and DEMO inquiry summaries stay in the browser; nothing is sent to YimiLife.
