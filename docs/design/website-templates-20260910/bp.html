PURCHASING QUESTIONS

Before you shortlist a model.

Compare the listed options and prepare the information for your project discussion.

What is the difference between YP7101, YP7201 and YP7202?

YP7101 uses an LED display, YP7201 uses an LCD display, and YP7202 uses an LCD display with backlight. Select a model to review its specifications and discuss your preferred display configuration.

Which cuff sizes are listed?

The listed adult cuff options are YM-C001A for an arm circumference of 22–32 cm and YM-C002A for 22–36 cm. Include the required cuff option in your project inquiry.

What memory and power specifications are listed?

All three models list voice readout and memory for two users with 120 records each. The listed power supply is a 3.7 V, 1200 mAh lithium battery with a Type-C input of 5 V, 1 A.

What information helps you review my OEM/ODM project?

Share the preferred model, cuff size, target market, expected quantity, and branding or packaging requirements. Include your target schedule and any specific documentation needs.

How are samples, minimum order quantities and delivery times agreed?

These are assessed for the selected model, cuff configuration and customization scope. Send your requirements and expected quantity so our team can discuss the sample plan and delivery conditions.
