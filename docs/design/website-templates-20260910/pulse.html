PURCHASING QUESTIONS

Before you shortlist a model.

Compare the listed options and prepare the information for your project discussion.

How do I choose a fingertip pulse oximeter model?

Start with the intended users and preferred product design, then compare the display, Bluetooth and buzzer configuration. YM603 is the listed pediatric model; the other listed fingertip models are for adults. Available options vary by model.

Which fingertip models offer Bluetooth?

Bluetooth is listed as optional for YM212, YM401, YM403, YM501 and YM503. Include your connectivity requirements when discussing the configuration for your selected model.

How do YH01 and YH02 differ, and which sensors are listed?

YH01 has a 3.5-inch LED display; YH02 has a 3.5-inch TFT display. Both list optional RS201 adult finger-sleeve, RS202 adult finger-clip and SS201 adult/pediatric nasal-alar sensors. Review the selected model’s specifications and battery-life conditions when defining your configuration.

What should I include in an OEM/ODM inquiry?

Share your preferred model, intended users, target market, display and connectivity requirements, expected quantity, and branding or packaging needs. For a handheld project, include your sensor requirements.

How are samples, minimum order quantities and lead times agreed?

These require a project review based on the selected model, configuration and customization scope. Share your expected quantity and target schedule so our team can discuss the sample plan and production conditions.
